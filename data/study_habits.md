# How I Study and Work



## My study routine
I usually study in the evening or at night. I prefer focused study sessions with short breaks to refresh my mind.

## How I learn
I learn best by watching tutorials, understanding concepts through examples, and practicing hands-on. For my projects, I usually start by understanding the requirements and breaking the work into smaller tasks.

## How I organize my work
I keep track of tasks using notes, checklists, tables, and project management tools like ClickUp.

## How I prepare for exams
I start by reviewing the syllabus, understanding important topics, making short notes, and practicing questions until I feel confident.

## How I work in a group
I usually take a coordinating and organizing role, helping divide tasks, communicate with team members, track progress, and make sure everything is completed on time.

## How I handle stress and deadlines
I prioritize important tasks, break large workloads into smaller steps, and focus on completing one thing at a time, even when I feel stressed.

## How I debug and solve problems
When something does not work, I try to understand the error, identify its possible cause, search for solutions, and test different approaches until I find a fix.

## Advice for junior students
Focus on understanding concepts rather than memorizing.
Start building practical projects early.
Be consistent and manage your time.
Explore different fields before choosing your specialization.
Never hesitate to ask questions or learn from mistakes.

## A mistake I learned from
Sometimes I spend too much time trying to make everything perfect before getting started, which has taught me to take action early, learn along the way, and improve gradually.
