## What cyber forensics is
Cyber forensics, also called digital forensics, is the process of identifying, collecting, preserving and analyzing digital evidence so that it can be trusted. Evidence can come from computers, phones, networks, cloud accounts and logs. An investigator must show that the evidence was not changed, so careful documentation and techniques such as hashing are important.
